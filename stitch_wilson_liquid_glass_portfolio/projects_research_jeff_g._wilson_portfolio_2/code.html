TrustRAG: Enhancing Reliability in Large Language Models through Graph-Augmented Retrieval

person Lead Author: Jeff G. Wilson

This paper introduces TrustRAG, a novel architectural framework designed to mitigate hallucination in generative AI models by integrating knowledge graphs directly into the retrieval pipeline. By enforcing semantic boundaries, the system achieves higher factual consistency in domain-specific querying scenarios.
